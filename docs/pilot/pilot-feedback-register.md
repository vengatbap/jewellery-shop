# Auric One ERP v1.0 — Pilot Feedback & Defect Register

## 1. Classification & Escalation Framework

All field feedback collected during the controlled pilot is categorized into one of five strictly defined buckets:

- **P0 — Critical Defect:** Business or checkout stopped. Immediate hotfix required.
- **P1 — Major Defect:** Significant workflow friction. Target resolution $< 24$ hours.
- **P2 — Usability Friction:** Minor UI or text alignment issue. Scheduled for v1.0.1 patch.
- **P3 — Enhancement Request:** Minor UX improvement idea. Logged to backlog.
- **Feature Request:** Out-of-scope module request. Deferred to v1.1.0 release cycle.
