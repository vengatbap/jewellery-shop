## Governance Rules

1. **Frozen Status:** The Auric One Architecture is declared **FROZEN** at Version 1.0.5.
2. **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs):** Any deviation or significant addition to the principles, boundaries, or topologies defined in this specification requires a formal ADR document to be drafted under `docs/architecture/adr/ADR-XXXX-description.md`.
3. **Review Board:** Changes to this architecture must be reviewed and approved by the Architecture Board before implementation.
