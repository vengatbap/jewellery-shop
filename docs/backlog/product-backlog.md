# Auric One Product Backlog

This backlog tracks system feature implementation priorities.

---

## 1. NOW (Active & Next Up)
These modules form the core MVP release of the platform:
* **Platform Security:** JWT verification, multi-tenant middleware, and append-only audit trail logging.
* **Masters Directory:** Product metadata, categories, metals, purities, daily gold rate history, customer database, and supplier profiles.
* **Inventory Management:** Alphanumeric barcode generation, stock entry logs, and inter-branch inventory transfers.
* **Billing POS:** Pricing engine (combining gross/net weights, making charges, wastage, and taxes), invoice creation, and payment collection.

---

## 2. NEXT (Targeting Beta Release)
These modules are scheduled immediately after the core transaction flow is verified:
* **Accounting Integration:** Automatic double-entry journal postings generated from POS invoices and payment receipts.
* **Savings Schemes:** Customer plans enrollment, monthly installment receipts tracking, gold rate lock policies, and maturity redemption discounts.
* **Reports & Analytics:** Cash flow registers, outstanding installment lists, and stock valuation audits.

---

## 3. LATER (Targeting Enterprise Release)
Advanced business modules:
* **Repair Management:** Tracking customer jewellery items submitted for polishing or resizing, assigning karigars, and recording repair charges.
* **Manufacturing Module:** Allocating raw gold weight to artisans, tracking melting logs, monitoring karigar metal balances, and logging wastage.
* **Pawn / Gold Loans:** Metal purity appraisal, loan agreement generation, interest accruals, and pledge/unpledge movements.
* **External Integrations:** E-commerce shop sync APIs, WhatsApp notifications, and third-party accounting imports.
